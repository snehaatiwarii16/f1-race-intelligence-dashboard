# Data dictionary
- `Record_ID`: synthetic row identifier
- `Season`: illustrative season year (2010–2026)
- `Driver`, `Nationality`, `Team`: illustrative driver/team attributes
- `Grand_Prix`, `Host_Country`: event labels
- `Tyre_Compound`: Soft, Medium, Hard, Intermediate
- `Finish_Status`: Finished or DNF
- `Failure_Type`: illustrative failure category
- `Finish_Position`: illustrative position, blank for DNF
- `Points`: illustrative points, not official scoring
